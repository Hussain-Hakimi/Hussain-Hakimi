## 🚀 About Me

> *"I don't just build spreadsheets — I build decision-making engines."*

I'm a **Software Engineering student** at the **University of Engineering and Technology (UET), Lahore** *(2025–2029)*. My journey began in Business Administration, but I pivoted to Software Engineering because I believe **code is the most powerful tool** to solve real-world problems.

Today, I bridge the gap between **data** and **design**:
- 📊 Turning raw data into **dynamic dashboards**, automated reports, and scalable data models
- 🎨 Building **clean, responsive web applications** from the ground up
- 🏗️ Architecting **desktop solutions** with solid OOP foundations and normalized databases

I'm an **Associate Developer** with a strong base in **C#**, **SQL**, and **Python**, actively growing toward **backend & full-stack development**.
